Q+A Katha - Marriage 🧠

Question: In "Resident Evil 3", how many inventory slots does Jill have at the start of the game?

Answer: 8 - General Knowledge. Itha kelvi Marriage pathi - Answer 8. Marriage pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Marriage pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Marriage oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Marriage ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Marriage la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
Q+A Katha - Family 🧠

Question: In the anime Assassination Classroom what is the class that Korosensei teaches?

Answer: Class 3-E - Entertainment: Japanese Anime & Manga. Itha kelvi Family pathi - Answer Class 3-E. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Family pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Family la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Family oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
